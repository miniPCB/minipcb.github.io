Equations, stress definitions, and assumptions

Rb = R2 || RL (or R2 with no load)

Vout = Vin × Rb / (R1 + Rb)

I1 = (Vin − Vout) / R1
I2 = Vout / R2
IL = Vout / RL (0 with no load)

P = I² × R
Rth = R1 || R2 (load excluded)

Pstress = P / Prated × 100%

Vlimit = √(Prated × R), capped by Vmax when entered.
Ilimit = Vlimit / R

Vstress = |V| / Vlimit × 100%
Istress = |I| / Ilimit × 100%

Nominal DC operating point with independent tolerance intervals for VIN, R1, R2, and RL. Temperature derating, transients, and independent datasheet current limits are not modeled.

Inverse solutions use g = VOUT / VIN, Rb = RTOP × g / (1 − g), RTOP = Rb × (1 − g) / g, and RBOT = Rb / (1 − Rb / RL) when loaded. A finite positive divider needs 0 < g < 1. RL may be open when the target equals the unloaded output.

Tolerance bounds evaluate the endpoint VIN values with minimum gain (R1 high, R2/RL low) and maximum gain (R1 low, R2/RL high). Common R1/R2 tolerance is solved by bisection to six decimal places in percent; a nonzero nominal VOUT is required.

Working voltage follows the lower of the power-derived voltage and the component's maximum working voltage. See Vishay's resistor technical guide.

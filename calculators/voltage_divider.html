Equations, stress definitions, and assumptions

Rb = R2 || RL (or R2 with no load)

VMID = VTOP × Rb / (R1 + Rb)

I1 = (VTOP − VMID) / R1
I2 = VMID / R2
IL = VMID / RL (0 with no load)

P = I² × R
Rth = R1 || R2 (load excluded)

Pstress = P / Prated × 100%

Vlimit = √(Prated × R), capped by Vmax when entered.
Ilimit = Vlimit / R

Vstress = |V| / Vlimit × 100%
Istress = |I| / Ilimit × 100%

Nominal DC operating point with independent tolerance intervals for VTOP, R1, R2, and RL. Catalog parts use the allowed power at their selected ambient; manual ratings are used as entered. Transients and independent datasheet current limits are not modeled.

Inverse solutions use g = VMID / VTOP, Rb = RTOP × g / (1 − g), RTOP = Rb × (1 − g) / g, and RBOT = Rb / (1 − Rb / RL) when loaded. A finite positive divider needs 0 < g < 1. RL may be open when the target equals the unloaded output.

Tolerance bounds evaluate the endpoint VTOP values with minimum gain (R1 high, R2/RL low) and maximum gain (R1 low, R2/RL high). Resistor-pair searches require this entire interval to fit the allowed band around the target VMID. Pair searches also evaluate worst-case power and voltage, including interior resistance values where power peaks; the other analysis tabs show nominal stress.

Multiple-part arms support a series chain or parallel bank. Each part has its own rating, tolerance, and optional working voltage. Equivalent resistance is the series sum or reciprocal conductance sum. Series parts share arm current; parallel parts share arm voltage. Solving RTOP/RBOT scales that arm's part resistances proportionally; it does not select standard values. The pair search selects one resistor per arm. Manual multi-part analysis remains available; ratings are not pooled into a fictitious arm rating.

Working voltage follows the lower of the power-derived voltage and the component's maximum working voltage. See Vishay's resistor technical guide.

Equations, stress definitions, and assumptions

Rb = R2 || RL (or R2 with no load)

Vout = Vin × Rb / (R1 + Rb)

I1 = (Vin − Vout) / R1
I2 = Vout / R2
IL = Vout / RL (0 with no load)

P = I² × R
Rth = R1 || R2 (load excluded)

Pstress = P / Prated × 100%

Vlimit = √(Prated × R), capped by Vmax when entered.
Ilimit = Vlimit / R

Vstress = |V| / Vlimit × 100%
Istress = |I| / Ilimit × 100%

Ideal resistors, an ideal DC source, and nominal resistance values. Temperature derating, tolerance, transients, and any independent datasheet current limit are not modeled.

Working voltage follows the lower of the power-derived voltage and the component's maximum working voltage. See Vishay's resistor technical guide.
